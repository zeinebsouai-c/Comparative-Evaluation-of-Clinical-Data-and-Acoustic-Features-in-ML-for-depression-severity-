The eGeMAPS extraction command used to live here as a copy-paste snippet with
absolute paths. It is now a runnable, path-independent script:

    scripts/extract_egemaps.ps1         clean resampled WAVs -> egemaps_features.csv
    scripts/extract_noisy_egemaps.ps1   noisy WAVs           -> egemaps_features_noisy.csv

Run either from anywhere:

    & "<repo>/audio_lanzhou_2015/scripts/extract_egemaps.ps1"

Both derive openSMILE and the audio root from the script's own location, and
honour the $env:OPENSMILE_ROOT / $env:MODMA_DATA_DIR overrides.
